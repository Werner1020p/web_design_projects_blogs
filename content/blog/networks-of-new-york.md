After reading Networks of New York, I started to see the city differently. The reading explains how digital networks are connected to physical infrastructure such as buildings, underground cables, and communication systems throughout the city. It shows that the internet and communication technologies are not separate from the physical world, they are built into it.

One idea that stood out to me is that cities like New York are full of layers of infrastructure that most people never see. There are networks under streets, inside buildings, and across neighborhoods that allow communication to happen instantly.

This reading made me think about how dependent we are on these systems. Every time we send a message, use maps, or stream media, we rely on networks that we rarely think about. It made me realize that modern life depends heavily on infrastructure that is easy to take for granted.

Personally, this reading changed how I look at the environment around me. I walk through the city every day without thinking about what is happening underground or behind walls. Now I understand that there are many hidden systems supporting everyday communication.

Overall, this reading helped me understand that cities are not just physical spaces but also complex technological networks. It also made me wonder how these systems will continue to grow as cities become more connected and technology keeps advancing.
